My approach

Most processes don't need AI everywhere. Using it everywhere makes them slower, more expensive and harder to trust. Using it nowhere leaves the judgment calls to people. My work is drawing that line precisely.

1 · Analyse

Map the process

I break the workflow down step by step and look at the inputs, rules and decisions behind each one.

2 · Split

Deterministic or AI?

Steps with clear rules become plain, tested code. Steps that need judgment over messy or open-ended input become AI evaluations and runtime decisions.

3 · Build

Automate the rules

The deterministic part is built as ordinary software: predictable, fast, cheap to run and easy to test.

4 · Guard

Constrain the AI

Every AI decision sits behind validators, deterministic checks, human review where it matters, and an audit trail.

I come to AI from more than ten years of building and leading software teams, which is what lets me fit it into real codebases, delivery processes and teams rather than into demos. I'm based in Italy and work remotely across the EU.
